Wrist

Tracks reaching height — how high each player's hand gets during the jump. Pure measure of arm extension at peak.

Hip

Center of mass — the purest measure of overall body elevation off the floor. Accounts for starting squat position.

Ankle

How far feet leave the ground — direct measure of explosive jump power. Unaffected by height or arm length.
